## Full description

Lily Bloom brings living water gardens to Minecraft. Grow flowers in four colours, discover three stages of bloom and decorate ponds with lily pads from a single block up to 4×4. Find them in swamps and mangrove swamps, or grow them with bone meal.

Version 2.0.0 adds nocturnal lilies, glow-coated flowers, four leaf shapes, floating candles and lanterns, whole-plant transplantation and rare speckled or striped petals obtained through breeding. Built-in 32× and 64× textures also cover the new shapes and patterns.

Lily Bloom aims to stand out through careful visual quality, coherent models and textures, useful survival interactions and attention to compatibility. English and Russian item tooltips make special traits easy to recognise.

### Gardening

Use bone meal to grow buds and shears to harvest fully open flowers. Sneak with shears to change a leaf's shape. Apply Lunar Pollen to make a lily nocturnal; use honeycomb to restore its daytime habit. Glow ink sacs make flowers emit light, while ordinary ink sacs remove the glow coating.

### Floating lights

An empty medium, large or huge pad can hold an undyed candle, lantern or soul lantern. Sneak with an empty hand to pick the light back up. Flowers and lights share the centre of the leaf.

### Breeding and transplantation

Use bone meal on an open flower with an open flower of a different colour nearby, on the same water level and within six blocks along each horizontal axis. The offspring inherits a parent's colour, leaf shape and nocturnal habit. Each offspring has a 1-in-8 mutation roll for speckled or striped petals; patterns can also be inherited. Glow coating is applied separately.

A Lily Transplanter moves a complete plant while keeping its flower, growth stage, traits and installed light. Craft it from a copper ingot and a stick placed diagonally. Craft four Lunar Pollen from an amethyst shard and a Blue Water Lily.

### Installation

Minecraft Java 26.3, Java 25, Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ and Fabric API 0.163.0+26.3 or a newer release for 26.3. Install Lily Bloom on the client for single-player, and on the server and every client for multiplayer. Use the same mod version on both sides. Back up existing worlds before updating.
